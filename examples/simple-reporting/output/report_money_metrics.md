# Report on money metrics

This report summarizes the financial performance and money-related metrics for L’Oréal based on the provided data, focusing on revenue distribution, growth, and strategic investments.

### **Financial Overview**

L’Oréal demonstrates strong financial health with a total revenue exceeding €43 billion and a high operating margin. The company maintains a balanced growth profile across earnings and sales.

| Metric                       | Value          |
| :--------------------------- | :------------- |
| **Total Revenue**            | €43.48 billion |
| **Operating Result**         | €8.69 billion  |
| **Operating Margin**         | 20%            |
| **Revenue Growth**           | +5.1%          |
| **Earnings Per Share (EPS)** | €12.66         |
| **EPS Growth**               | +4.8%          |

### **Revenue by Business Department**

The revenue is diversified across four main divisions. The **Consumer Products** (Produits Grand Public) and **Luxe** divisions are the primary financial drivers, accounting for 73% of total revenue.

| Department                                      | Revenue Share (%) |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| Produits Grand Public (Consumer Products)       | 37%               |
| Luxe                                            | 36%               |
| Beauté Dermatologique (Dermatological Beauty)   | 16%               |
| Produits Professionnels (Professional Products) | 11%               |

### **Geographic Revenue Distribution**

L’Oréal maintains a global presence in 150 countries. **Europe** and **North America** remain the strongest markets, together contributing 60% of total revenue.

| Region                           | Revenue Share (%) |
| :------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| Europe                           | 33%               |
| Amérique du Nord (North America) | 27%               |
| Asie du Nord (North Asia)        | 24%               |
| SAPMENA — SSA                    | 9%                |
| Amérique latine (Latin America)  | 8%                |

### **Digital and Innovation Investment**

L’Oréal allocates significant capital toward research and digital transformation, which is reflected in their high e-commerce penetration.

- **E-commerce Revenue:** €12.3 billion (representing **28.2%** of total revenue).
- **Research Investment:** Over **€1 billion** annually.
- **Social Investment:** **€70 million** dedicated to the "L’Oréal Fund for Women" (Fonds L’Oréal pour les Femmes).

### **Key Insights**

- **Digital Leadership:** With nearly 30% of revenue coming from e-commerce, digital sales are a critical pillar of L’Oréal's financial strategy.
- **High Profitability:** An operating margin of 20% indicates high operational efficiency and strong pricing power across its 37 international brands.
- **Strategic R&D:** The investment of over €1 billion in research, resulting in 694 patents filed, suggests a strategy of long-term value creation through innovation.
